CareClaim Health Insurance Claim System

An enterprise end-to-end medical claim adjudication ecosystem featuring three dedicated, synchronized web portals for Patients, Claims Audit Officers, and Administrators.

Launch Patient Portal Launch Officer Portal Launch Admin Portal
👤
Portal A

Patient / Policyholder Portal

Designed for policyholders to enroll, manage insurance policies, file itemized hospital claims, and track live status.

Included Features:
  • User Registration & Login: Account creation, email/password login, forgot password.
  • Policy Management: Coverage details, policy validity, remaining balance bar.
  • Submit New Claim: Patient details, hospital records, expenses breakdown, document upload.
  • Track Claim Status: 6-stage tracker (Submitted, Under Review, Docs Required, Approved, Rejected, Payment Completed).
  • Real-Time Notifications: Status updates, missing document alerts, payment confirmations.

🩺
Portal C

Claim Officer Portal

Clinical audit workstation for designated healthcare adjudicators to inspect hospital invoices and recommend outcomes.

Included Features:
  • Assigned Claims Queue: Dedicated triage for claims assigned to this officer.
  • Verify Uploaded Documents: Mark verified or flag discrepancies with auditor notes.
  • Check Details & Expenses: Audit hospitalization stay and itemized expenses.
  • Request Additional Documents: Triggers "Documents Required" status & patient alert.
  • Recommend Approval / Rejection: Submit proposed settlement amounts & clinical notes.
  • Patient Remarks: Direct notes visible on the patient's tracker.
Open Officer Portal →
🛡️
Portal B

Admin Management Portal

Master administrative console for policy configuration, officer assignment, final claim payouts, and reporting.

Included Features:
  • Admin Authentication: Secure administrative login and role controls.
  • Registry of Users: View all registered policyholders, officers, and credentials.
  • Master Claims List: Complete visibility into all submitted claims.
  • Assign to Officers: Direct workflow routing to specific claim adjudicators.
  • Approve, Reject & Settle: Calculate deductibles, disburse funds, deduct policy balance.
  • Manage Policies: Create and edit insurance products and benefits.
  • Audit Reports & CSV: Executive analytics, KPI dashboards, and CSV export.

Interactive Lifecycle Verification

  1. Submit Claim: Open Patient Portal and file a new claim with itemized expenses & invoices.
  2. Assign Officer: Open Admin Portal → Claims Queue → Assign claim to Dr. Michael Chang.
  3. Audit & Request Documents: Open Officer Portal → Inspect uploaded bills → Request missing pharmacy invoice.
  4. Patient Notification: Switch back to Patient Portal → Observe the Documents Required warning banner → Upload missing files.
  5. Recommend & Final Payout: In Officer Portal recommend approval → In Admin Portal click Adjudicate → Click Authorize Payout & Mark Payment Completed.
  6. Settlement Complete: Observe the patient's policy remaining balance instantly deduct and payment confirmation receipt generated!
